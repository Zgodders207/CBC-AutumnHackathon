Who it’s for

Commuters and families on one Gateshead estate who can’t easily get to a bike shop during opening hours.

What you sell

A home visit for common repairs, priced as a call-out fee plus parts, booked on two set evenings a week, with a winter service plan for regulars.

How you’ll find customers

Free safety checks at a community event, then word of mouth on one estate before spreading to the next.

Where to get proper advice

Speak to an accountant about tax before your first paid job, and check your council’s rules on trading from home or in public places.
